# User Story: demand_signal_instead_of_user_research

**ID:** US-14

## Story

As a product manager or solo builder,
I want the community to rank my roadmap directly,
so that I can skip recruiting, surveys, and hypothesis interviews for every feature idea.

## Acceptance criteria

- Demand is measured by actual votes from actual users, continuously — not by a one-off study.
- The signal is available *before* the feature is built, so validation happens pre-build rather than post-release.
- No separate research tooling, panel recruitment, or interview scheduling is required.
- The ranked list is exportable/readable as the roadmap itself, not as an input someone must re-interpret.

## Notes

Outcome story. Transcript: "instead of recruiting people and running hypothesis interviews… the community gives you the signal, and you don't have to rely on market research."
